How to read it

Quantum reservoir: 5 qubits, one per input (wind toward the sector, ISI, today's advance, BUI, humidity), last 5 days, fixed circuit; only a ridge read-out is trained.
ESN: classical reservoir, 20 units. Ridge: the same inputs fed straight into the read-out.
FBP: Canada's fire-behaviour spread rate (uses fuel type). Persistence: today's advance repeated.
All models were trained on 2018–2021 only and settings were chosen on 2022, so 2018–2021 are seen data, 2022 is the validation year and 2023 is the untouched test year.
